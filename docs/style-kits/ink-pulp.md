# Ink Pulp

Best for weird arcade, dark comedy, comic action and monster-heavy games.

## Visual rules
- Thick black ink contours and aggressive silhouettes.
- Off-white paper or dirty flat-color backgrounds.
- One or two hard accent colors, often red/orange/olive.
- Use halftone, dry-brush, photocopy noise and print misregistration.
- Compose moments like panels, captions and impact frames rather than app cards.

## Typography
Condensed grotesk, oversized display caps, rough captions and large impact words. Keep body copy minimal.

## Motion
Hard cuts, snap zooms, short panel shakes, speed lines and smear frames.

## Reusable vocabulary
Bursts, captions, speed lines, stamps, panel borders, rough icons, print textures and ink splats.

## Avoid
Soft pastel glow, glass surfaces, tiny hairline icons and generic cyber HUDs.
