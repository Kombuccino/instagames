# Fuggy — Canonical MiniFugg Mascot

This file is the source-of-truth contract for the MiniFugg mascot **Fuggy**. Read it before creating, rendering, posing, animating, modelling or integrating Fuggy anywhere in MiniFugg.

## Canonical identity

Fuggy is the exact mascot family approved by the user on 2026-09-06. Do not redesign Fuggy from memory and do not substitute another gremlin/cat/robot/creature style.

## Locked visual traits

Fuggy must preserve these recognizable traits:

- compact, friendly, low-poly / faceted 3D construction;
- large faceted off-white head and compact off-white body;
- two coral-red triangular ears;
- a wide, dark charcoal/black visor-like face mask;
- two small bright white diamond-shaped eyes inside the mask;
- very restrained facial features; no large mouth, teeth, fangs or horror expression;
- short simple arms and legs;
- chibi-like compact proportions, approximately 2.5 head-heights tall in the approved reference;
- simple silhouette that remains recognizable at small size;
- modern, playful and slightly odd, but never creepy, sinister or aggressive.

## Palette

Reference palette from the approved board:

- body / bone: `#F7F4ED`
- face mask / charcoal: `#1E1F21`
- ears / coral red: `#FF5A4A`
- soft shadow neutral: `#D9D6CF`
- dark brand background: `#0F1114`

Minor lighting variation is allowed, but the identity should still read as the same off-white / charcoal / coral mascot.

## Expression language

Fuggy's personality comes mostly from head tilt, body pose and the two white eye diamonds.

Allowed examples:

- neutral;
- curious;
- happy;
- surprised;
- mischievous/playful;
- peeking, waving, walking, sitting, jumping.

The eye language may temporarily simplify the two white diamonds into tiny **kaomoji / Japanese-emoticon-like marks** inside the same black visor. This is canonical for animated Fuggy expressions as long as it remains restrained and eye-only. Useful examples include:

- `^^` for happy / pleased;
- `--` or `==` for sleepy / dozing;
- `><` for a tight blink, excitement or a playful squeeze;
- `oo` for suddenly awake / surprised;
- shifted small diamonds or marks for looking left/right.

These marks replace the visible eye diamonds for the duration of the expression; do not leave the fixed white eyes visible underneath an animated expression. Do not add a mouth to complete the emoticon.

Avoid turning Fuggy into a conventional cartoon face with detailed eyebrows, human lips, exposed teeth or large expressive pupils. The mask + restrained white eye marks are the primary face grammar.

## 360° / scene consistency

When generating Fuggy from a new angle, use the canonical reference board as a model sheet rather than reinterpreting the character independently in each image.

The approved reference board contains front, 3/4, side, back, opposite side, 3/4 back, pose and head studies. New scenes may change lighting, material treatment, pose and camera angle, but must keep the same underlying character geometry.

## 3D master status

There is currently **no approved 3D master of Fuggy**.

Until a 3D model is explicitly approved, the only geometry references are the Affinity source, the extracted user-reference PNG and the approved 360° board.

When a final 3D model is explicitly approved:

1. freeze its proportions and named parts as the 3D master;
2. preserve an unrigged neutral-pose master;
3. derive rigged/animated variants from that neutral master;
4. keep GLB as the preferred runtime-friendly format where practical;
5. preserve source/editable DCC files separately from runtime exports;
6. update this document with the exact master filenames/version.

If a generated Fuggy looks like a different mascot, discard it. Consistency is more important than novelty.
