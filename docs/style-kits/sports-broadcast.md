# Sports Broadcast

Best for racing, sports, timing games, reflex challenges and score chases.

## Visual rules
- Think TV graphics, race timing and arena scoreboards rather than a game menu.
- Strong horizontal bands, lane markings, badges and compact status strips.
- High-contrast black/white or dark/cream base with one loud accent color.
- Numbers are heroes: big, tabular and instantly readable.
- Decorative elements should usually communicate status, speed, rank or timing.

## Motion
Fast directional wipes, score ticks, lap/split changes and short data transitions. Keep bounce minimal unless the sport itself calls for it.

## Reusable vocabulary
Timer, lap, split, rank, lane, flag, badge, ticker, checkpoint and position marker.

## Avoid
Fantasy ornament, soft glass cards, ambient glow without information and tiny score text.
