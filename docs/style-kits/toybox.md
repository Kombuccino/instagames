# Toybox

Best for physics, stacking, party games and direct manipulation of objects.

## Visual rules
- Chunky toy-like silhouettes with strong readable volume.
- Bright but controlled primary/secondary colors plus warm neutral backgrounds.
- Materials should feel like painted wood, plastic, foam or rubber.
- Use simple pseudo-3D shading and highlights rather than glossy glass effects.
- Objects should be oversized enough to read instantly on a phone.

## Motion
Weight matters: anticipation, bounce, squash, wobble and clear collision response. Motion can be playful but must communicate physics.

## Reusable vocabulary
Blocks, balls, crates, pegs, springs, platforms, wheels, buttons, ramps and chunky handles.

## Avoid
Thin abstract interfaces, micro typography, dark cyberpunk palettes and decorative complexity that makes objects harder to grab.
