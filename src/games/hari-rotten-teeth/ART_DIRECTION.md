# HARI les dents pourries — Art Direction

Base kit: **Ink Pulp**.

## Intent
A grotesque candy/dentist pulp-comic panel: ugly funny teeth, dirty printed paper, hard black outlines and impact-caption energy. It should feel like a cheap horror comic crossed with an arcade cabinet, not like MiniFugg Core UI.

## Palette
- Dirty paper / tobacco beige background.
- Near-black ink outlines and offset shadows.
- Gum / warning red as the main accent.
- Sickly olive board.
- Candies keep five saturated, highly distinguishable flat colors.

## Geometry and texture
- Thick 3–4px ink contours.
- Slight rotations and misregistration instead of perfect app-card alignment.
- Sparse halftone / photocopy noise.
- Teeth are deliberately irregular in size, tilt and silhouette.

## Motion
- Short stepped/punchy candy pulses.
- Matches shoot candy projectiles upward toward the corresponding tooth.
- Level clear drops all rotten teeth like a comic impact gag.

## UI constraints
- Essential gameplay stays between `--minifugg-core-top-reserved` and `--minifugg-core-bottom-reserved`.
- Controls are chunky printed buttons with black offset shadows, not glassmorphism.
- No duplicate MiniFugg score, title, replay or leaderboard UI inside the game.
