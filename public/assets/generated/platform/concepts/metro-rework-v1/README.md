# Metro entry composition studies — 12 September 2026

Unvalidated composition studies generated locally with the built-in image tool from the current wide metro pilot screenshot. Each source PNG has a companion `-mobile-guide.png`; the cyan rectangle is the `390 × 844` portrait MASTER at the same image height.

## Options

- `option-1-frontale`: clearest seated relationship and calmest perspective. Strong desktop read, but the reader and phone do not both fit inside the portrait crop without moving them closer.
- `option-2-oblique`: most populated and natural carriage. It preserves moderate depth but also separates the reader and phone too much for one mobile crop.
- `option-3-immersive`: intentionally wide first-person view. It gives the clearest foreground/midground/background split for parallax and brings reader and phone closest inside the portrait crop. The wide-angle effect must remain restrained in production.

## Shared constraints

- clean warm low-poly rendering;
- player clearly seated, with a complete connected forearm;
- reader seated with believable posture;
- no Fuggy on the bench;
- a small number of passengers and lived-in details;
- no photorealism or dirty paper treatment;
- phone/hand, carriage, and exterior city separable for animation.

The generated phone and hand are composition placeholders. Production keeps the approved MiniFugg phone content and the selected reusable hand assets.
