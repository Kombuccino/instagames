# Paper Cut

Best for puzzles, sorting, cozy games, food/craft and casual manipulation.

## Visual rules
- Build objects as layered paper/cardboard/felt silhouettes.
- Use warm off-white backgrounds and a small family of printed colors.
- Slightly imperfect edges and tiny registration offsets are welcome.
- Shadows should feel like stacked paper, not glassmorphism.
- Labels can feel stamped, taped or hand-lettered, but must stay readable.

## Motion
- Lift objects slightly when grabbed.
- Use short paper flips, springy placement and tactile drag feedback.
- Avoid floaty sci-fi easing.

## Reusable vocabulary
Stickers, labels, trays, cards, tape, stamps, paper tokens, cut shapes and simple cardboard containers.

## Good customizations
Change the craft material (paper, felt, cardboard, fabric), print palette and illustration subject while keeping the tactile layered construction.
