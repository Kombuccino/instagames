# Editorial Grid

Best for numbers, logic, word games, abstract strategy and board-like puzzles.

## Visual rules
- Strong grid, clear rules, columns, circles and typographic blocks.
- Favor print/editorial composition over floating cards.
- Use off-white/black plus one or two print-like accent colors.
- Large type and spacing are part of the visual identity, not an afterthought.
- Diagrams and marks should feel intentional and precise.

## Motion
Precise slides, number flips, line draws, cursor movement and restrained emphasis. Avoid gratuitous particles.

## Reusable vocabulary
Grid, rule, index, marker, number, label, diagram, stamp, coordinate and annotation.

## Avoid
Neon sci-fi, excessive rounded containers, decorative bloom and tiny gray labels.
